FAQs

Questions people ask us

Which treatment should I choose?

U-Lipolysis suits stubborn fat pockets when you're close to your goal weight. GLP-1 suits overall weight loss. If you're unsure, choose "Need a suggestion" and our specialist will recommend one at your free consultation.

Does U-Lipolysis hurt?

No. It's non-invasive with no needles or anaesthesia. Most people feel mild warmth or a light buzzing during the session.

When will I see U-Lipolysis results?

Many clients notice first inch loss after 1–3 sessions, with visible contouring in 3–6 weeks. Results vary by individual.

How does the GLP-1 programme work?

GLP-1 helps regulate appetite, so you feel full for longer and eat less. Our specialist plans your programme, tracks your progress and adjusts it as you go.

Is GLP-1 right for everyone?

No. Our specialist reviews your health history at the consultation to check if GLP-1 is suitable for you before you start.
